## Verify Your Purchased Sets

Use this checklist before ordering any top-ups:

- **Diodes:** confirm you have at least four 1N4007 rectifiers and enough 1N5819 Schottky diodes for the buck, boost, and motor circuits. Read the part markings; generic assortment contents are not interchangeable by appearance.
- **Capacitors:** confirm two 100 uF / 10 V parts for Lab 06, two 220 uF / 16 V parts for Lab 07, at least two 100 nF bypass capacitors, and 100 uF / 25 V plus 470 uF / 25 V for Lab 09. The 1 uF inverter-filter part must be film and at least 25 V.
- **Inductors:** count the Swpeet kit's 1 mH parts and verify three meet all power ratings above. The LCR meter verifies value (and perhaps DCR), not saturation current or SRF. The separate RS 100 uH parts do not count.
- **Resistors:** your received RS order covers five each of 10, 22, 47 and 100 ohm / 5 W. Check your assortment for 150 ohm, 1 k ohm / 0.5 W, 2.2 k ohm, 10 k ohm and 100 k ohm. Do not buy top-ups for values you already have.
